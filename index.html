Massage Therapy

Price varies  ·  60 min and up

At Silver Linings Massage, I offer therapeutic massage designed to help you find relief from physical tension and pain caused by stress, poor posture, and everyday life. Whether you are just looking to relax, or find relief from pain or tension, I incorporate different tools and techniques for the best therapeutic outcome. Some of these include trigger point therapy, Graston myofascial scraping, and hot stones. Massage can also help you find relief from headaches, calm the nervous system, improve circulation, help the body relax and recover after a strenuous workout, promote better sleep, and support overall wellbeing. Each session is tailored to your individual needs, creating a supportive environment where your body can heal, reset, and feel its best.

Craniosacral Therapy

45 min  ·  $70

Do you ever feel wired but tired? Can't fall asleep at night? Craniosacral therapy is a deeply relaxing, gentle, hands on therapy that uses light touch to release tension in the body's connective tissues, or fascial linings, surrounding the brain and spinal cord, known as the craniosacral system. This gentle, noninvasive therapy is intended to improve the flow of cerebrospinal fluid and restore balance to the central nervous system and enhance the body's natural healing abilities, potentially easing chronic pain, headaches, and stress related issues.
